
# Tarliam Global

A business website developed for **Tarliam Global**, a trading and sourcing company focused on connecting businesses with reliable products and suppliers.

## Overview

This project involved designing and developing a professional business website to establish Tarliam Global's online presence and provide potential clients with information about the company's services.

## Features

* Responsive business website
* Company and service information
* Product/sourcing presentation
* Contact and inquiry form
* Mobile-friendly design
* SEO and search engine setup
* Business email integration

## Technologies & Services

* HTML
* CSS
* JavaScript
* Netlify
* Formspree
* Zoho Mail
* Google Search Console

## My Role

Designed and developed the website from the ground up, including the website structure, user interface, content presentation, deployment, contact form integration, and basic search engine setup.
